Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EcoRound Holsters ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit www.ecoroundholsters.com or place an order. By using our site, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Order details: your name, shipping address, email address, phone number, and the products and configuration options (firearm model, finish, etc.) you select.
  • Custom-graphic artwork you send us by email for a personalized order.
  • Email address if you join our updates/"Notify Me" list.
  • Messages you send us (e.g., support or warranty emails).

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical/log data your browser sends (such as IP address and device/browser type) when you load the site.
  • A shopping cart saved in your browser's local storage so your selections persist between pages. This stays on your device.

Payments

Card payments are processed by Stripe. Your full card number is entered on Stripe's secure checkout and is never seen or stored on our servers. Stripe's handling of your data is governed by the Stripe Privacy Policy.

How we use your information

  • To process, build, and ship your order, and to contact you about it.
  • To create custom graphics from artwork you provide.
  • To send product news and offers if you opted in — you can unsubscribe anytime.
  • To respond to your questions and warranty requests.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, and to comply with the law.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers we rely on to run the store:

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Google — business email (Google Workspace) and a spreadsheet that stores email-list signups (Google Sheets / Apps Script).
  • Railway — website hosting.
  • Shipping carriers (e.g., USPS) — to deliver your order.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

Email list

If you sign up for updates, your email is stored in a private Google Sheet. To be removed, use the unsubscribe option in any email or contact us at info@ecoroundholsters.com.

Cookies & analytics

With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site (pages viewed, approximate location, device type). For this, Google Analytics sets cookies. On your first visit we show a banner and analytics cookies load only if you choose "Accept" — you can decline, and either way essential cart storage still works. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy, and you can opt out site-wide with Google's browser opt-out add-on.

Data retention

We keep order information as long as needed to fulfill your order, provide warranty support, and meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or opt out of marketing emails, by emailing info@ecoroundholsters.com. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

Children

Our store is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, and payments run over encrypted connections through Stripe. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version.

Questions about your privacy?

Email us at info@ecoroundholsters.com.
